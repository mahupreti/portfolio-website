What makes Broom different

Cleanup that respects your time and your privacy.

Broom is built for real cleanup sessions, not just a quick novelty swipe. Pause, come back, and keep making progress.

Your library stays yours

Everything happens on your phone. No account, no upload, no Broom server.

Free, without a paywall

Core cleanup is free, with ads. Watch a short ad if you need extra swipes in a day.

Progress you can pick up

Sorting progress resumes when you return, even with a large library.

Deletion on your terms

Nothing disappears until you review your choices and confirm the sweep.
